# Administrator Operations Manual

## 1. Overview
This runbook provides administrative operational guidelines for managing BPS Twin Towers Community PWA.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### A. Resident Onboarding & Approvals
- Navigate to `/admin` → **Tab 1: Registrations**.
- Inspect flat assignment and uploaded parking/occupancy proof.
- Click **Approve** (assigns flat membership) or **Request Correction** (with mandatory feedback note).

### B. Event & Festival Coordination
- Navigate to **Tab 4: Events**.
- Create event dates, pooja slots, and participant limits.
- Publish event to make it visible on resident portals (`/events`).

### C. Community Finance & Donations
- Navigate to **Tab 5: Finance**.
- Review submitted donor transaction references against bank statements.
- Click **Verify** to confirm donation receipts and update campaign progress.

### D. Security & Gate Operations
- Gate staff operate from `/security` (high-contrast console).
- Verify pass code (`BPS-XXXX`) or search by vehicle/flat number.
- Log walk-in entries or check in expected guests.
- Admins can inspect the real-time **Emergency Occupancy Manifest** in **Tab 9**.

### E. Facility Bookings & Maintenance
- Navigate to **Tab 10: Facilities**.
- Approve or reject pending bookings with feedback.
- Click **Schedule Block** to close sports courts or pool for scheduled cleaning/repairs.

### F. Helpdesk & Complaints Triage
- Navigate to **Tab 11: Helpdesk**.
- Monitor live counters for **Urgent** tickets and **Overdue** SLAs.
- Assign tickets to maintenance departments (Plumbing, Electrical, Housekeeping).
- Enter resolution summary upon repair completion.
