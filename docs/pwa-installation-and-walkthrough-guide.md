# 📱 Step-by-Step PWA Installation & App Walkthrough Guide

## Part 1: How to Install the App ("Add to Home Screen")

### 📱 On Android (Google Chrome / Brave)
1. **Open the App Link**: Open the application URL in Google Chrome or Brave.
2. **Bottom Banner Prompt**: A prompt titled *"Add to Home Screen — Tap to install as a mobile app"* appears at the bottom.
3. **Tap Install**: Click the blue **Install** button on the banner.
4. **Confirm**: Tap **Install** or **Add** on the browser confirmation popup.
5. **Done**: The **BPS Twin Towers** icon will appear on your phone's home screen and app drawer, opening in full-screen standalone mode without any browser URL bar.

> **💡 Tip**: If the bottom banner was closed (✕), tap the **3 vertical dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner of Chrome and select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.

---

### 🍏 On iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)
1. **Open in Safari**: Open the app URL in Safari.
2. **Tap Share Icon**: Tap the **Share** button (⎋ / square with an arrow pointing up `📤`) at the bottom center of Safari.
3. **Select "Add to Home Screen"**: Scroll down the menu options and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (`➕`).
4. **Confirm**: Tap **Add** in the top right corner.
5. **Done**: The app icon is added to your iPhone home screen with offline caching enabled.

---

### 💻 On Laptop / PC (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge)
1. **Address Bar Icon**: Look at the right side of the browser URL/address bar for the **Install App icon** (desktop monitor with download arrow).
2. **Or Bottom Banner**: Click the **Install** button on the bottom banner.
3. **Confirm**: Click **Install** in the popup.
4. **Done**: The app launches in its own native standalone window with a desktop shortcut created.

---

## Part 2: Feature Walkthrough (Step-by-Step)

### 1️⃣ Viewing Day-wise Pooja & Cultural Schedule
* On the **Festival Home** tab, tap **📅 Pooja Schedule** under Quick Actions.
* Filter across all 6 days (**Day 1 to Day 6**) to view Muhurtham times, Mantras, SPOC / Lead contacts, and Live Archana details.

### 2️⃣ Checking Maha Prasadam (Dynamic & Consistent Menu)
* Tap **🍲 Prasadam** on the bottom navigation or quick action card.
* View **Day 1 through Day 6** menus cleanly formatted with dish icons, timings, context, and sponsors.
* Tap **🔄 Sync** at any time to pull real-time updates directly from the master Google Sheet.

### 3️⃣ Making a Contribution
* From **Festival Home**, tap the **🤎 My Contribution** status card or the **Contribute / Pay** button.
* Select your **Flat number** and contribution amount.
* Scan the generated QR code or copy the UPI ID to pay instantly.

### 4️⃣ Festival Team Contacts
* Tap the **👥 Team** tab on the bottom navigation bar.
* Access all volunteer coordinators, committee leads, and emergency festival contacts.
